Privacy

A short note on how this personal site works — not a company policy.

Who runs this

This site is operated by Jy Yeüng at thisisjyyeung.com. Works shown here are © Jy Yeüng; all rights reserved.

What I collect

There is no account system and basically no first-party data collection today. Pages are static. I don’t ask you to sign up or leave cookies of my own for tracking.

Third-party embeds

Some pages (for example Dysphoria) may embed players such as Apple Music. Those embeds can set their own cookies or process data under their policies — not mine. If you prefer not to load them, leave those pages or block third-party content in your browser.

Contact form (when live)

When the form on the Contact page goes live, it will send your name, email, and message through Formspree. Formspree processes that submission under their terms; I use what you send only to reply. You’ll get an email receipt when it sends. I usually reply within 5–7 days. I may not respond to spam or hostile messages.

Analytics

No first-party analytics today. I may use privacy-respecting analytics later (for example Plausible or Umami). If that happens, this page will be updated. Prefer cookieless / privacy-first tools so a cookie-consent wall isn’t required.

Cookies

This is an essential/static artist site. There’s no cookie consent banner for first-party essentials, because there aren’t any tracking cookies from me. If non-essential cookies or analytics that need a notice are added later, I’ll update this Privacy page and — only if needed — add a simple notice. Preference: privacy-first analytics that doesn’t need a CMP banner.

Questions

Privacy questions: use the Contact page when the form is live, or reach out on Instagram @thisisjyyeung.

Updates

I may update this note as the site changes. The date in the footer © line tracks the current year for the site.
